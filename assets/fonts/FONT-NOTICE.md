# Editorial fonts

Satoshi, Boska and Switzer are supplied by Indian Type Foundry through
Fontshare. The included WOFF2 files are unmodified webfonts.

Font information and applicable licensing:
- https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/satoshi
- https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/boska
- https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/switzer
- https://www.fontshare.com/licenses

All three are declared in `editorial.css` and all three ship in this directory.
Switzer was originally missing from this notice while its four weights were
being served, which is a notice narrower than what it covers.

The original portfolio's IBM Plex Sans and JetBrains Mono assets and license
notices are retained in this directory.
