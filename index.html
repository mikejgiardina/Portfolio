ENGINE

Computational Physiology Engine

Evidence-Based Physiology, Computed in Real Time

Clinical Problem Every clinical tool downstream — simulators, training, decision support — needs a source of truth for how a body behaves over time. Off-the-shelf approaches don't give you a physiology you can compute, audit, and build on.

A physiologically grounded engine that computes a patient's trajectory from underlying mechanisms, cellular to system level. Vitals, labs, and drug response evolve over time, grounded in published physiology and pharmacology. Every case presentation is distinct, and the underlying state is fully auditable.

  • Multi-agent, physiologically grounded architecture
  • Physiology-driven behavior — not hard-coded scripts
  • GPU-accelerated batch computation
  • Evidence-based pharmacokinetic library
  • Clinical scoring: SOFA, qSOFA, NEWS2, GCS
  • Core ML surrogate export for on-device inference
  • Provisional patent filed February 2026

SIMULATOR

Body Simulator

Clinical Simulator Front-End

Clinical Problem Bedside-quality patient simulators are locked into expensive vendor mannequins tied to a single training room. Every discipline that trains on patients needs a software-only simulator driven by real physiology, not scripted playback.

A clinical front-end wired live to the physiology engine over a WebSocket bridge. Vitals, four-channel waveforms, and drug response render from computed engine state in real time, with a graceful fallback when the bridge is offline.

How the Work Gets Built Safely
