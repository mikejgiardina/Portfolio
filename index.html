Multi-Agent Physiological Simulation Engine

Clinical Problem Clinical simulation platforms are either low-fidelity or prohibitively expensive. Educators, researchers, and developers need a physiologically grounded engine where complex clinical behavior is driven by underlying mechanisms — not by scripted scenarios.

A physiologically grounded multi-agent simulation engine spanning cellular to system levels, where complex clinical behavior is driven by underlying physiology rather than scripted scenarios. Async Python/PyTorch architecture with GPU-accelerated batch computation and a Core ML surrogate path for on-device inference.
